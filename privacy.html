This Privacy Policy explains what personal information is collected when you use my portfolio website and app (together, the “Service”), how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Who is responsible

The Service is run by Peeyush Kumar Jangid, an individual HVAC Project Engineer based in Jaipur, India (“I”, “me”). For any privacy question, write to peeyush.jangid@outlook.com.

2. Information I collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication, I never see or store it in readable form), and your mobile number.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google”, I receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
  • Verification data: your email verification status and the one-time password (OTP) sent to your mobile number.
  • Job offer and job application forms: the details you type in, such as name, designation, company, email, phone, job role, location, salary, message, and, for applications, your city, experience, availability and the CV you upload or link.
  • App activity: files you open, download or save inside the app, and the star rating you submit.
  • Sign-in records: each time you sign up or sign in, the sign-in method (email or Google), the time, and basic device information from your browser (the user-agent text) are saved so I can see who has registered and keep the Service secure.
  • Technical data: information your browser shares automatically, and data stored on your device to run the app, such as cached files and sign-in state.

I do not knowingly collect sensitive data such as payment card details, government ID numbers, or precise location.

3. How I use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to verify your email and mobile number.
  • To send me an email alert when a new account is created or a form is submitted.
  • To show your profile, downloads and saved items inside the app.
  • To receive job offers and job applications, and to contact you about them by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To improve the Service and keep it secure and free from misuse.

I do not sell your personal information and I do not use it for third-party advertising.

The Service relies on third-party providers that process data on my behalf or when you use them directly:

These providers have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for their practices.
