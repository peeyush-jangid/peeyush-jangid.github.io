How I work on a site

A billing engineer connects what is drawn, what is installed and what is invoiced. This is the sequence I follow on HVAC projects.

  1. Measure

    Take site measurements for completed work.

  2. Draw

    Prepare VRV drawings in AutoCAD 2D.

  3. Supervise

    Follow installation, ducting and chilled water piping.

  4. Bill

    Prepare running and final bills in Excel.

  5. Document

    Keep reports and pre-commissioning records.
