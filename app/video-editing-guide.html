Video editing seekho: pehle cut se pro grading tak

Level 1

Beginner: basics pakkay karo

Kuch bhi edit karne se pehle ye 7 cheezein samajh lo.

1Video editing kya hai aur kaam kaise hota hai

Video editing ka matlab hai alag-alag raw clips ko kaat-jodkar ek kahani banana: awaaz, music, text aur colour ke saath. Har edit lagbhag isi order mein hota hai:

  1. Import
  2. Rough cut
  3. Fine cut
  4. Audio
  5. Colour
  6. Titles
  7. Export
  • Import: clips ko folder mein rakho (Raw, Music, Graphics) aur software mein laao.
  • Rough cut: sirf kaam ke hisse timeline par rakho, order sahi karo.
  • Fine cut: har clip ki shuruaat aur ant trim karo, pause hatao.
  • Audio, Colour, Titles: awaaz saaf karo, colour theek karo, text lagao.
  • Export: final video file banao.
Tip: Pehle kahani, phir effects. Achha cut kisi bhi transition se zyada kaam karta hai.

3Editor screen ke 5 hisse
  • Media Bin: aapka saaman ka godown.
  • Preview: jo timeline par hai wo yahan dikhta hai.
  • Inspector: selected clip ki settings.
  • Timeline: clips ko order mein lagane ki jagah.
4Timeline, tracks aur pehla cut

Pehla cut, 5 step

  1. Clip ko timeline par drag karo.
  2. Playhead (laal line) wahan rakho jahan cut chahiye.
  3. Split dabao: Ctrl+B (CapCut), Ctrl+K (Premiere), Ctrl+\ (DaVinci).
  4. Bekar hissa select karke Delete dabao.
  5. Beech ka khali gap close karo (ripple delete ya drag).

Trim: clip ke kinare ko pakad kar andar kheencho, to shuruaat ya ant chhota ho jaata hai. Original footage kharab nahi hota.

Tip: Space se play/pause aur Ctrl+Z se undo. Darne ki zarurat nahi, editing mein sab kuch wapas hota hai.
5Shot types aur framing
  • Wide: scene kahan hai. Shuruaat mein use karo.
  • Medium: insaan aur uska action.
  • Close-up: chehra, cheez ki detail, emotion.
  • Rule of thirds: subject ko grid ke crossing point par rakho, bilkul beech mein nahi.
Tip: Cut on action: jab koi haath uthaye ya mudde, usi movement ke beech cut lagao. Cut dikhta hi nahi.
6Audio aur music ki basics

Viewer kharab picture maaf kar deta hai, kharab awaaz nahi. Isliye awaaz pe pehle kaam karo.

  • Clipping: awaaz laal zone mein jaaye to phat-phat sunai deti hai, baad mein theek nahi hoti.
  • Royalty-free music: copyright claim se bachne ke liye free music libraries use karo (jaise YouTube Audio Library) aur license padho.
  • Fade in / fade out: music ke shuru aur ant mein 1-2 second ka fade lagao.
7Text lagao aur pehli video export karo
  • Text bada aur saaf rakho, background se alag dikhe. Maximum 2 fonts.
  • Text ko screen ke kinare se thoda andar rakho, taaki phone mein kat na jaaye.
  • Ek baar mein 6-8 shabd se zyada nahi, padhne ka time do.

Level 2

Intermediate: video ko professional banao

Ab cuts ko smooth, awaaz ko clean aur colour ko theek karna seekhte hain.

8Story, hook aur pacing
  • Hook: pehle 3 second mein batao ki video mein kya milega. Intro baad mein.
  • Structure: Hook, problem, solution, call to action.
  • Pace: short videos mein har 2-5 second mein nayi shot ya angle badlo.
  • Silence hatao: "umm" aur lambe pause kaato. Video turant tight lagta hai.
  • B-roll: jump cut ko chhupane ke liye upar relevant footage lagao.
  • Beat par cut: music ke beat par cut karo, rhythm achha lagta hai.
Tip: Final cut dekho aur jahan bore ho jao, wahan se 20% kaat do.
9J-cut aur L-cut

Pro editors awaaz aur video ko alag-alag time par badalte hain, isse conversation smooth lagti hai.

Kaise karein: video aur audio ko unlink karo, phir sirf audio ke kinare ko thoda aage ya peeche drag karo. Interview aur vlog mein sabse zyada kaam aata hai.

10Transitions aur speed
  • Cut: 90% jagah yahi sahi hai.
  • Dissolve: time guzarna ya mood badalna dikhane ke liye.
  • Zoom / whip: energy wale vlogs aur reels mein, kam use karo.
  • Speed up: boring kaam ko tez karo. Slow motion: achhi quality ke liye 60 fps ya zyada shoot kiya hona chahiye, warna jhatka lagta hai.
  • Speed ramp: beech mein speed ko dhire se badlo, action shots mein bahut mast lagta hai.
  • Freeze frame: ek frame rok kar text ya label dikhao.
Ek video mein 4-5 alag transition lagana amateur lagta hai. Ek ya do style chuno aur wahi repeat karo.
11Audio cleanup aur ducking
  1. Noise reduction: hiss aur fan ki awaaz kam karo, par zyada nahi, warna awaaz robot jaisi ho jaati hai.
  2. High-pass EQ: lagbhag 80 Hz se neeche ki gadgadahat kaat do.
  3. Compressor: tez aur dheemi awaaz ka fark kam karta hai.
  4. Ducking: jab koi bole to music apne aap halka ho jaye.
Tip: Hamesha headphones se sunke mix karo, phone speaker se bhi ek baar check karo.
12Colour correction

Correction ka matlab hai footage ko sahi dikhana. Grading (mood banana) baad mein aata hai. Order ye rakho:

  • Pehle exposure: kaale bilkul kaale aur safed bilkul safed na ho jayein.
  • Phir white balance: safed cheez safed dikhe, skin tone natural ho.
  • Aakhir mein saturation: thoda sa badhao, overdo mat karo.
  • Sab clips par ek jaisa look rakho, warna video chhalang maarta hua lagta hai.
13Aspect ratio aur platform formats
  • Vertical video mein text ko upar aur neeche ke kinaro se door rakho, wahan app ke buttons aur caption aate hain (lagbhag 15-20% hissa).
  • Horizontal clip ko vertical project mein daalo to blur background ya zoom/crop use karo, kaali patti nahi.
Level 3

Advanced: studio level kaam

Animation, colour grading, compositing aur proper workflow.

14Keyframes aur animation

Keyframe ek nishaan hai jo bataata hai "is time par value ye honi chahiye". Do keyframe ke beech software khud animation bana deta hai. Size, position, rotation, opacity sab animate hota hai.

  1. Time 0 par keyframe lagao (jaise Scale 100%).
  2. Playhead aage le jao, value badlo (Scale 120%). Naya keyframe apne aap bann jaata hai.
  3. Keyframe par right-click karke Ease in / out chuno.
  4. Graph editor khol kar curve ko haath se adjust karo.
Use cases: slow zoom-in, text slide, logo fade, picture-in-picture ka move, Ken Burns effect photo par.
15Colour grading: scopes, LUT aur look

Grading mein footage ko mood milta hai (cinematic, warm, cold). Ye correction ke baad hota hai.

  • Scopes: waveform (roshni), vectorscope (rang), histogram. Aankh dhokha de sakti hai, scopes nahi.
  • Log footage: flat dikhta hai. Pehle log ko normal (Rec.709) mein badlo (LUT ya colour management se), phir grade karo.
  • LUT: ready-made look file. Hamesha 30-50% strength par lagao, phir apne hisaab se tweak karo.
  • Nodes (DaVinci): Node 1 correction, Node 2 look, Node 3 vignette ya skin touch-up. Har kaam alag, taaki ek ko band karna aasaan ho.
  • Skin tone: vectorscope par skin line ke aas-paas rakho.
16Masking, tracking aur green screen
  • Mask: clip ke ek hisse par effect lagao (sirf aasman ko dark karo).
  • Motion tracking: text ya sticker ko chalte hue object ke saath chipkao.
  • Rotoscoping: frame-by-frame subject ko background se alag karna.
  • Adjustment layer: ek layer jo neeche ke sabhi clips par effect lagaye.

Green screen (chroma key) ke steps

  1. Shoot mein green kapda sapaat aur ek jaisa roshan rakho, jhurriyan na hon.
  2. Subject ko background se door khada karo, taaki hari parchhai na aaye.
  3. Keyer effect lagao aur green rang pick karo.
  4. Edge softness aur spill suppression badhao (kinaron par hara halo hatane ke liye).
  5. Naya background piche rakho aur colour match karo.
17Pro workflow: proxy, codecs, frame rate

4K footage heavy hota hai aur laptop atakta hai. Proxy workflow ye fix karta hai:

  1. 4K original
  2. Chhota proxy banao
  3. Proxy par edit
  4. Original se relink
  5. Export

Frame rate aur shutter

  • 24 fps: cinematic. 25 fps: India/PAL TV. 30 fps: online video. 50-60 fps: sports aur slow motion.
  • 180 degree rule: shutter speed ko frame rate ka double rakho (25 fps par 1/50) to motion blur natural lagta hai.
  • Multicam: alag cameras ko audio waveform se sync karo, phir live angle switch karke cut lagao.
18Sound design, loudness aur career
  • Layers: dialogue, music, sound effects (whoosh, click), aur ambience (hawa, sadak ka background). Ambience se video asli lagta hai.
  • Whoosh har transition par nahi, sirf mazboot moment par.

Pro habits

  • Folder structure: 01_Raw, 02_Audio, 03_Graphics, 04_Project, 05_Export.
  • Backup 3-2-1: 3 copy, 2 alag device, 1 bahar (cloud ya dusri jagah).
  • Presets aur templates: apne text style, colour look aur intro ko save karo, time bachta hai.
  • Portfolio: 60 second ki showreel, achhe kaam ke 5-6 clips ke saath.
  • Kaam kahan milta hai: YouTubers, reels creators, shaadi aur events, company ki corporate videos. Pehle chhote local clients se shuru karo.
  • Aage seekhne ke liye: motion graphics (After Effects ya Fusion) aur sound mixing.

Practice

30 din ka seekhne ka plan

✓Har hafte ka kaam
  1. Hafta 1, basics: 5 alag clips ka 30 second ka vlog banao. Sirf cut, trim aur music lagao. Export karo.
  2. Hafta 2, awaaz aur text: apni voice record karo, noise reduction aur ducking lagao, titles daalo.
  3. Hafta 3, reel: 9:16 mein 20 second ka reel banao. Hook, B-roll, J/L cut, speed ramp aur basic colour correction.
  4. Hafta 4, project: 3 minute ki short video. Keyframe animation, ek colour grade, loudness check aur sahi export settings.
Seekhne ka sabse tez tareeka: koi video pasand aaye to usse frame-by-frame dekho aur khud copy karke banao. Har baar ek nayi cheez seekho, sab ek saath nahi.
