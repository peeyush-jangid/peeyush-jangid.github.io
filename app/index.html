Engineer suite

Which AC tonnage do I need?

Enter your room details to get an estimated AC size.

BTU/hr, kW and TR converter

Type a value in any one box. The others update.

1 TR = 12,000 BTU/hr = 3.517 kW. Marketing "1.5 ton" split ACs are usually rated about 5 kW cooling.

Duct size from airflow

Get round and rectangular duct sizes for a given airflow.

Rule of thumb: about 400 CFM per TR of cooling. Final duct design needs static pressure and layout checks by an engineer.

Electrical details: current, breaker, cable

Enter the load to get full-load current, MCB rating and cable size for an AC, pump, fan or any motor.

Indicative values for PVC cable in air or tray at 40 °C, resistive voltage drop only. Compressors need a motor-rated breaker and starter. Always confirm with the local electrical code and a licensed electrician.

Detailed cooling load

Room heat gain from walls, roof, glass, people, lights, equipment and fresh air. Defaults suit Jaipur summer.

Envelope

Internal loads and fresh air

Uses steady-state U × A × ΔT with solar adders, ASHRAE-style people gains (75 W sensible, 55 W latent) and 0.3 L/s/m² added to fresh air. Good for pre-design and quotations; final design should use a full CLTD or hourly method.

Psychrometric calculator

Find wet bulb, dew point, humidity ratio, enthalpy and volume from dry bulb and RH.

Magnus saturation formula and ASHRAE equations. Jaipur is about 431 m above sea level.

Chilled water pipe and pump sizing

Flow rate, pipe size, friction loss and pump power for a chilled water circuit.

Hazen-Williams with C = 120 for steel, nominal bore used as inside diameter, 1.5× run length for fittings. Keep velocity near 1.2–2.5 m/s, and lower in occupied areas.

AC lifetime cost comparison

Compare two ACs over their life. A costlier efficient AC often wins on total cost.

Use average running power, not the label's maximum. Inverter units usually run well below rated power.

Duct static pressure and fan power

Total external static pressure for one duct run, then the fan motor power it needs.

Darcy-Weisbach with Swamee-Jain friction (galvanised steel, roughness 0.09 mm, air 1.2 kg/m³). Elbow K = 0.25 for smooth radius elbows. Add 10–15% margin for dirty filters and site changes.

EER, COP and kW per TR

Type any one value. The others update, and you get a yearly running cost.

Yearly running cost

COP = EER ÷ 3.412 and kW/TR = 3.517 ÷ COP. Assumes full load. Part load and weather change real use.

Fan and pump VFD savings

Affinity laws: flow ∝ speed, pressure ∝ speed², power ∝ speed³. Slowing a fan 20% cuts its power by about half.

Compares full speed with the VFD at the new speed. Real systems with static head or a fixed pressure setpoint save somewhat less.
