Duct static pressure

Size a round duct for a friction rate and get the total static pressure the fan must overcome.

Darcy-Weisbach with galvanised steel roughness 0.09 mm, air at 1.2 kg/m³. A bend is about K 0.2–0.5, a tee about 0.5–1.0. Add the fan's own inlet/outlet effects separately.

EER, COP and kW per TR

Enter cooling capacity and electrical input to rate an AC, VRV or chiller.

COP = cooling kW ÷ input kW. EER (BTU/hr per W) = COP × 3.412. kW/TR = 3.517 ÷ COP. For split ACs also compare the ISEER on the BEE label, which covers part-load running.

Fan and pump VFD savings

Compare a throttled or damper-controlled motor with a variable frequency drive using the affinity laws.

Load profile (flow or speed % and share of time)

Power with a VFD ≈ (flow)³ ÷ 0.97 drive efficiency. Without a VFD the motor runs at constant speed with a damper or valve, taken as 50% + 50% × flow. Pumps with high static head save less than this shows.
